Task 4

Identify an animal model for cognition

Find a tool entry for an animal model related to NF1-associated cognitive features.

Explore: tools
Switch to the Tools tab and select the Resource Type, Animal Model of Manifestation, and Animal Model of Genetic Disorder search filters. Filter for terms like Animal Model, Cognition, and Neurofibromatosis type 1.

Model name, Synapse ID, or URL

About this workshop

The NF Data Portal (nf.synapse.org) is a Synapse-powered platform aggregating NF1, NF2, and schwannomatosis datasets from research consortia worldwide. You may need a Synapse account to request access to controlled datasets.
